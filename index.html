FAQs

Questions owners ask us

Do I need a machine room?

No. Where a machine room is not possible, we mount a gearless motor directly on a structural beam above the shaft, saving space without compromising safety or comfort.

Which motor is right for my building?

Geared motors suit mid-rise buildings and budgets. Gearless motors are best for high-rise and luxury projects. Belt drive is a modern rope-free alternative. We confirm the choice after a site survey.

What shaft size do I need?

It depends on capacity. A 4 person lift needs a 1500 × 1500 mm shaft, and a 13 person lift needs 2100 × 1900 mm. See the dimensions table above for all sizes.

Can the cabin be customised?

Yes. Choose gold mirror, SS hairline or rose gold mirror finishes, ceiling designs, handrails and door styles. Fully custom cabins with art and patterns are available virtually.

What does after-sales service include?

24/7 support, preventive maintenance, emergency response, genuine spare parts, certified technicians, AMC plans and optional remote monitoring.

Can I restrict who uses the lift?

Yes. Access control options include RFID cards, passwords, fingerprint and face recognition, alone or in combination.
